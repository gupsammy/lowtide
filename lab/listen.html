lowtide listening lab

Ten tracks in radio order, as the critic lets them through. "Play all" plays each track in full, then the next, as the radio will; click a waveform to jump within a track. "Openings in a row" plays the first 8 seconds of each back to back: if two sound alike, the engine has failed. Rate with 👍 or 👎 and any tags that fit; ratings stay in this browser until you export them. Each card's riff buttons play the same track with the riff on the keys, on the lead's sound or on a kalimba; • marks the role the track drew, and a faded role is one the fit rules wouldn't draw for it. The track plays in full or as a beat tape, without the lead; • marks the version the track drew. Moves turns off the parts dropping out and back, and the riff's double, to hear the track without them. The strip over the waveform shows who plays each bar: bright for the full band, middle without drums, dim for the loop alone.
